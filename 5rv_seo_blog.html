The 6 Real Reasons Your SEO Generates Reports but Not Revenue
01

Targeting traffic volume instead of buyer intent

Ranking for "what is SEO" attracts researchers. Ranking for an intent-led term attracts buyers ready to call. Most agencies optimise for volume because it is easier to show progress on a vanity dashboard. Demand to see the keywords driving actual form completions and phone calls.

02

Your website is not built to convert SEO traffic

SEO gets people to your site. If your pages do not convert, every visit is wasted. Common killers: no phone number above the fold, page load over 3 seconds, no trust signals, generic CTAs. 33% of companies underinvest in CRO — creating a permanent gap no ranking improvement will close.

03

Your agency is measuring vanity metrics, not revenue indicators

If your report leads with rankings and ends there, it is a vanity report. Your monthly report should show organic enquiries, revenue from organic search, keywords driving conversions, GBP performance, and month-on-month lead trend — not traffic trend.

04

Your Google Business Profile is being ignored

A fully optimised GBP generates at least 200 profile views and 15–25 direct calls per month within 6 months. AI Overviews now appear for nearly 9% of transactional keywords. If your agency has never mentioned GBP in a report, your local SEO is being neglected entirely.

05

No alignment between SEO, social media, and branding

Three separate suppliers gives you three strategies that do not talk to each other. 60% of marketers confirm SEO generates the highest quality leads — but only when supported by a brand that builds immediate credibility.

06

Your campaign has not been localised

National tactics applied to a location-specific business produce national traffic that never converts locally. A properly localised Birmingham campaign needs location-specific landing pages, GBP optimisation for the local pack, citation building, and review generation across the West Midlands.

⚑

Red flag: Clients do not renew SEO contracts based on ranking improvements. They renew based on business growth. If your current agency cannot show you enquiry numbers, ask why — today.

What Good SEO Reporting Actually Looks Like in 2026

Most businesses in the West Midlands have never seen a proper revenue-focused SEO report. Here is exactly what it should show every single month:

The 6 metrics your report must include

Organic enquiries — form completions and calls attributed to organic search in GA4
Revenue from organic channel — actual value of leads or sales, not impressions
Keywords driving conversions — only the terms generating real enquiries
Google Business Profile performance — views, calls, and direction requests
Core Web Vitals status — page speed and mobile performance scores
Month-on-month lead trend — not traffic trend. Lead trend.

If your report does not show all six, request an immediate restructure.
