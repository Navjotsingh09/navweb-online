Your monthly report shows keyword rankings climbing, impressions up 40%, traffic growing week on week — yet your phone has not rung from a new customer in three months. This is more common than most agencies will admit.

> A business getting 3,000 monthly organic visitors and zero enquiries is not an SEO success story. It is an SEO consultancy that optimised for the wrong thing.

Rankings and revenue are not equivalent. One client holds the top spot in the local pack yet sees flat revenue. Another drops in rank but grows revenue by 40%. If your consultancy is not tracking enquiry numbers, the issue lies with their strategy — not your traffic.

## The conversion gap, in numbers

Industry benchmarks suggest organic search converts at around **2.4%** on average. That gives you a simple sanity check:

| Monthly visitors | Expected enquiries (~2.4%) | If you're seeing zero |
|---|---|---|
| 500 | ~12 | Problem exists |
| 1,000 | ~24 | Problem exists |
| 3,000 | ~72 | Serious problem |

If the right column describes you, the gap is not in your rankings. It's somewhere between the click and the enquiry.

## The 6 real reasons your SEO generates reports but not revenue

**1. Targeting traffic volume instead of buyer intent.**
Ranking for "what is SEO" attracts researchers. Ranking for an intent-led term ("SEO agency Birmingham", "emergency plumber Solihull") attracts buyers ready to call. Most agencies optimise for volume because it is easier to show progress on a vanity dashboard. Demand to see the keywords driving actual form completions and phone calls.

**2. Your website is not built to convert SEO traffic.**
SEO gets people to your site. If your pages do not convert, every visit is wasted. Common killers: no phone number above the fold, page load over 3 seconds, no trust signals, generic CTAs. Industry data suggests roughly a third of companies underinvest in conversion rate optimisation — creating a permanent gap no ranking improvement will close.

**3. Your agency is measuring vanity metrics, not revenue indicators.**
If your report leads with rankings and ends there, it is a vanity report. Your monthly report should show organic enquiries, revenue from organic search, keywords driving conversions, GBP performance, and month-on-month lead trend — not traffic trend.

**4. Your Google Business Profile is being ignored.**
A fully optimised Google Business Profile typically generates hundreds of profile views and dozens of direct calls per month within six months. AI Overviews now appear for an increasing share of transactional queries. If your agency has never mentioned GBP in a report, your local SEO is being neglected entirely.

**5. No alignment between SEO, social media, and branding.**
Three separate suppliers gives you three strategies that do not talk to each other. SEO traffic converts best when it lands on a brand that builds immediate credibility — that's a coordination problem, not a channel problem.

**6. Your campaign has not been localised.**
National tactics applied to a location-specific business produce national traffic that never converts locally. A properly localised Birmingham campaign needs location-specific landing pages, GBP optimisation for the local pack, citation building, and review generation across the West Midlands.

> **Red flag.** Clients do not renew SEO contracts based on ranking improvements. They renew based on business growth. If your current agency cannot show you enquiry numbers, ask why — today.

## What good SEO reporting actually looks like in 2026

Most businesses in the West Midlands have never seen a proper revenue-focused SEO report. Here is exactly what it should show every single month:

1. **Organic enquiries** — form completions and calls attributed to organic search in GA4
2. **Revenue from organic channel** — actual value of leads or sales, not impressions
3. **Keywords driving conversions** — only the terms generating real enquiries
4. **Google Business Profile performance** — views, calls, and direction requests
5. **Core Web Vitals status** — page speed and mobile performance scores
6. **Month-on-month lead trend** — not traffic trend. Lead trend.

If your report does not show all six, request an immediate restructure.
